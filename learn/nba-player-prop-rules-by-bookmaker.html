What happens to an NBA player prop if the player doesn't play

At Neds, Ladbrokes and bet365, an NBA player prop is void and your stake comes back if the player doesn't take the court at all. Once he has played, even for a few seconds, the bet stands on his final numbers, and overtime counts. Sportsbet's basketball rules aren't public: its help articles on them need a Sportsbet login. What Sportsbet does publish is its Fair Go Refund, which refunds a losing pre-game bet in cash if the player suffers a game-ending injury in the first half. TAB's published rules don't say what happens to a basketball over/under prop when the player doesn't play, so check with TAB before you bet.

Read from each bookmaker's published help pages on 27 November 2026. Rules and promotions change, and they differ by state and by market. The bookmaker's own terms on the bet slip always apply. Links to each source are at the bottom.

The short version

  • Ruled out before the game: void, stake returned, at Neds/Ladbrokes and bet365. TAB: only clearly refunded in group markets with eight or more selections, such as Most Points. Sportsbet: not publicly available.
  • Plays, then gets injured or ejected: the bet stands on what he did at Neds/Ladbrokes and bet365. A player who goes off after two minutes counts as having played. Sportsbet's Fair Go Refund (until 11 April 2027) refunds losing pre-game bets when the injury is game-ending and happens in the first half. Ejections aren't covered.
  • Doesn't start but comes off the bench: stands. Starting only matters for markets like first basket.
  • Overtime: counts towards the total. At TAB, look for IncOT ("includes overtime") in the market name.
  • Same game multi with a void leg: at TAB, Neds and Ladbrokes the whole same game multi is void and the stake refunded. At bet365 a basketball same game multi with a void player leg is recalculated on the remaining legs.

The TAB gap

TAB's published refunds policy is the odd one out. Its default is "all-in": a bet on a competitor who is scratched or withdrawn stands, unless the market has eight or more quoted selections, in which case a nominated player who doesn't take part is refunded. TAB also lists Most Points (Basketball) as a market that is always refunded. Its sports betting rules have a basketball section, but it covers only postponed and abandoned games. We couldn't find a rule written for basketball player points, rebounds or assists. Most TAB player props will probably be treated as void when the player doesn't play, but we can't show you where TAB says so. Ask TAB, or read the terms on the market itself, before relying on it.

The Sportsbet gap

Sportsbet's help articles on US basketball rules, injuries and ejections, same game multis with a voided leg, and player stats all ask you to sign in, so we can't show you its standard wording. Check the terms on the market in the Sportsbet app, or ask Sportsbet, before relying on how a void or a late scratch will be settled.

Injury refunds are promotions, not rules

Sportsbet's Fair Go Refund runs from 7 September 2026 to 11 April 2027 on NBA and NFL regular season games. If a player suffers a game-ending injury in the first half, Sportsbet refunds losing eligible bets on him in cash. Bets already lost before the injury aren't refunded. A same game multi isn't refunded if another leg had already lost. Live bets, bonus bets, Cash Out and ACT residents are excluded, and an ejection isn't an injury. Outside those terms, Sportsbet's standard rule decides, and that rule isn't public.

Why "takes the court" matters

A player listed as probable who's a late scratch gets your stake back. One who starts, plays two minutes and leaves with a sprained ankle usually doesn't, and an over on his points will almost certainly lose. That's why late team news matters so much for props, and why our predictions are rerun when a player is ruled out or a starting five changes, and again once the starters are confirmed.

Which numbers settle the bet

  • Sportsbet: not publicly available.
  • Ladbrokes (and Neds) settle on official NBA.com results.
  • bet365 settles on the stats published by the official competition site on the day of the game. Later amendments don't change settlement.
  • TAB settles on the result officially declared at the end of the event. Changes made after that are ignored (Rules 10.2 and 10.7).

The NBA corrects box scores for a day or two after a game. A rebound or assist taken off or added later can decide a bet that sat right on the line, and whether your bookmaker resettles depends on its rule above. We re-read every box score for three days after each game for the same reason.

How we grade our own

For The Drop and the graded props, a player who doesn't take the court makes the result void, the way the bookmakers treat it, and a void is never counted as covered or not. The track record counts voids apart from the wins and losses.
